# Surface brief: `/bookings` (provider calendar)

Mode: **Operate**  
World: Merchant Calendar Pad (locked)

## Job

Owner/staff scans the month, opens a day, acts on bookings.

## First viewport

Cool chrome header + tabs; status line; full-width **calendar pad** (board `#F7F3EA`) with today ring `#FF7A00`. No peach weekend gradients.

## Signature interaction

Day opens as a short tear-off sheet/modal; duration blocks in the day list use length as signal.

## Untouched

Booking business logic, permissions, E2E labels («сегодня», статусы записей).
